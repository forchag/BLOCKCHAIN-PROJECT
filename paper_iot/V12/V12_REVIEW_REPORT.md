# Version 12 eight-reviewer report

## R01: Editor and journal scope

**Assessment:** Suitable in topic and article form. The revised structure
matches the concise pattern observed in the repository's published journal
references. The problem, contribution, evidence, and practical implication
are now visible in the Introduction.

## R02: Experimental systems

**Assessment:** The field and controlled campaigns are separated. Hardware,
workload, counterbalancing, and experimental units remain explicit. Claims
about physical scale-out are excluded.

## R03: Statistics

**Assessment:** Runs remain the unit of inference. Confidence intervals,
blocked analyses, exploratory equivalence wording, diagnostics, and Holm
adjustment are retained.

## R04: Hyperledger Fabric

**Assessment:** The manuscript distinguishes endorsement, ordering,
validation, and commitment without assigning unmeasured stage latency.
“Observed peak” replaces capacity and saturation claims.

## R05: Security

**Assessment:** Scripted refusals are treated as conformance evidence for the
deployed implementation. The lack of positive requests and incomplete
independent-oracle coverage remain within scope.

## R06: Embedded systems and cryptography

**Assessment:** CRT recovery is evaluated against observed packed values and
all pairwise bounds. The corrected signature verifier follows the historical
firmware byte layout without claiming a standards migration.

## R07: Reproducibility and ethics

**Assessment:** Real-data provenance, historical and corrected artifacts,
privacy treatment, ethics, funding, CRediT, and competing-interest statements
remain explicit.

## R08: Language and production

**Assessment:** The title is shorter, the audit is integrated into Discussion,
the problem statement is explicit, repetitive prose is reduced, headings are
conventional, and captions contain one sentence of no more than 20 words.
The supplementary PDF contains detailed tables, supporting figures, and the
complete main-article figure set.

## Overall decision

V12 is substantially more concise and professionally structured than V11.
No verified numerical result was altered. Submission readiness remains
conditional on final PDF validation and the journal's upload checklist.
