# Version 12 change log

Version 12 is an editorial and structural revision of the verified Version 11
analysis. Numerical results and their evidence boundaries are unchanged.

## Main manuscript

- Shortened the title to remove implementation-audit terminology.
- Added a concise problem statement to the Introduction.
- Reduced the Introduction, contributions, audit narrative, and Conclusion.
- Integrated the implementation audit into the Discussion.
- Replaced the limitations heading with **Scope and validity**.
- Preserved all scientific qualifications as evidence boundaries.
- Removed em dashes and review-history language.
- Standardized the main structure to Introduction, Related work, System
  design, Implementation, Methodology, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion.
- Limited every table and figure caption to one sentence and at most 20 words.
- Retained run-level inference, confidence intervals, and the distinction
  between historical and corrected implementations.

## Supplementary material

- Retained protocol algorithms, detailed methods, operational notes, detailed
  tables, and supporting figures.
- Added the complete four-figure set from the main article.
- Preserved editable plot data and reproducible source files.

## Scientific interpretation

The revision introduces no new data and changes no verified result. It
improves readability and journal conformity while preserving four scope
boundaries: outage traces do not prove zero sensor-data loss, logical peer
multiplicity is not physical scale-out, observed peak is not a capacity
estimate, and corrected-code performance remains unmeasured.
