# Reference papers from *Internet of Things* (Elsevier)

Fifteen articles from the target journal, kept in the repository so the
manuscript's formatting and scope decisions can be checked against the
venue rather than against a general impression of it.

Observed conventions used to shape `hrbac_iot_cas.tex`:

- **Length**: 14 to 29 pages in the journal's double-column layout, median 17.
- **Structure**: five to eight numbered sections. Introduction, then
  related work or background, then the proposed system, then evaluation,
  then conclusion.
- **Figures**: 4 to 15 per paper (median 8). **Tables**: 1 to 9 (median 4).
- **Algorithms**: 1 to 8 in the eight papers that use them.
- **Citations**: numeric, square-bracket style.
- **Back matter**: CRediT statement, competing-interest declaration,
  acknowledgements, data availability, references.
