Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript "Blockchain access control for agricultural edge IoT: a 61-day Hyperledger Fabric field study and implementation postmortem" for publication in Internet of Things. The article reports a 61-day agricultural edge deployment, controlled latency and throughput campaigns, and an implementation postmortem grounded in released real measurements.

The revision separates field observations from controlled experiments and scripted conformance tests, uses the experimental run as the unit for campaign-level inference, and reports confidence intervals and bounded interpretations. It identifies three cross-layer defect classes in the deployed implementation and an outage-observability limitation. Historical performance is not presented as corrected-code performance, and the paper does not claim universal security, capacity or zero sensor-data loss.

The Version 11 artifact is available at https://github.com/forchag/project-fet2/tree/main/paper_iot/V11. All authors have approved the manuscript, and the work is not under consideration elsewhere.
