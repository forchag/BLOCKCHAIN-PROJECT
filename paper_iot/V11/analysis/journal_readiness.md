# Elsevier Internet of Things readiness checklist

- Scope: agricultural edge IoT, blockchain access control, field operations and
  reproducible systems evidence are stated without universal claims.
- Abstract: unstructured prose, bounded results and limitations; no Highlights
  page is embedded in the manuscript.
- Highlights: separate file, five bullets, each no more than 85 characters,
  following Elsevier's Internet of Things author guidance
  (https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/internet-of-things/publish/guide-for-authors).
- Methods: field deployment, controlled campaigns, experimental units, timing
  boundaries, outage observability and corrected-code separation are stated.
- Results: all primary values expand from generated macros and plot/table data.
- Declarations: funding, competing interest, ethics/data protection, CRediT,
  acknowledgements and generative-AI disclosure are present.
- Data availability: points to the actual repository and does not invent a
  future commit or DOI.
- Reproducibility: build command and raw-data/analysis paths are documented.
- Remaining author checks: confirm author names/emails/ORCIDs, ethics reference,
  permissions for public release, and final merged commit URL before submission.
