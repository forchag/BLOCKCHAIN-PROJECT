# Version 11 premortem

The manuscript was read forward from the likely reasons for a desk reject or
major-revision decision. Each issue is either corrected in the V11 source or
made explicit as a remaining evidence boundary.

1. Scope and novelty. The paper now presents one agricultural edge-IoT
deployment plus controlled campaigns and a postmortem. It does not market one
site as universal scalability or security proof.

2. Experimental design. The run, not the transaction, is the unit for
controlled campaigns. The peer campaign has 32 runs, eight per configuration,
with the recorded counterbalanced order; the throughput campaign has five runs
per condition and concurrency cell.

3. Statistics. V11 retains run-level estimates, 95% intervals, a post-data
exploratory TOST caveat, Holm correction, and non-significant interaction
wording. It does not convert repeated transactions into independent
replicates.

4. Hyperledger Fabric interpretation. Logical peer multiplicity on fixed
physical hosts is distinguished from physical scale-out. The observed peak is
not called capacity.

5. Security claims. The 8,000 records are scripted refusals through the
deployed denial mechanisms. The independent policy-oracle limitation, absence
of a permitted-request corpus, and lack of corrected-code performance remain.

6. Embedded and cryptographic audit. CRT recovery is reported against the
observed packed-value range, not a theoretical maximum. Quantisation error is
reported without a manufacturer-tolerance claim. Signature verification is
described as a legacy-compatible corrected verifier.

7. Data provenance, outage and ethics. Real field and controlled records are
identified, merged-folder provenance is stated, and the single-timestamp
outage contradiction is documented. V11 withdraws zero-data-loss language.
Funding, competing interest, ethics/data protection, CRediT and AI-use
statements are present.

8. Journal production. Highlights are a separate file with five bullets within
Elsevier's 85-character rule. The manuscript no longer embeds a Highlights
page, uses the actual repository path, includes declarations, and has a
reproducible build plus a separate supplement.

No new experiment is required to submit the manuscript if the authors accept
the stated limitations. Corrected-implementation performance and direct
capture/commit outage evidence remain follow-up work, not claims made by V11.
